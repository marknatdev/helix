Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy outlines how the HELIX Command Center collects, utilizes, and secures operational and telemetry data of connected smart helmets. Our primary objective is to safeguard industrial site personnel and optimize operational workflow efficiency.

Data We Collect

HELIX collects telemetry transmitted from registered smart helmets via a direct LoRa receiver gateway. This data includes:

  • Location Information: Real-time GPS coordinates (latitude, longitude, altitude) and heading/direction to monitor safety zones and respond to incidents.
  • Sensor Telemetry: Battery status, speed, and motion sensor triggers (such as impact/fall alerts).
  • Identity Mapping: Helmet serial IDs (such as HLX-001) linked to worker profiles, role designations, and assigned crews.

Purpose of Data Processing

The collected telemetry data is processed exclusively for the following safety and operational use cases:

  • Real-time situational awareness on the live interactive site map.
  • Instant alerts for fall detections, geofence breaches, and battery status.
  • Incident reports and analytics to assess site safety performance.
  • Crew and helmet allocation management.

Data Sharing and Privacy

We respect user privacy and adhere to the highest standard of data hygiene. We do not sell, rent, or trade telemetry data. Information is only visible to authorized project managers, safety coordinators, and command center administrators.

Security Standards

Telemetry and worker mapping data is stored securely in Firebase Firestore. We employ end-to-end encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and secure rule layers to verify that database reads and writes are restricted exclusively to authenticated site administrators.
